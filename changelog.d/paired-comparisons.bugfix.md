Make decoding comparisons trustworthy: a resumed unit is now checked against a fingerprint of its exact input data (not only its config), so a changed table or row set never reuses a stale fit; every decoding record carries its CV design, `n_samples`, `n_groups` and a `cohort_signature` of the samples it was scored on; the head-to-head report treats a group with missing or differing signatures as not comparable instead of computing unchecked deltas or reporting a false `nan | nan | nan` mismatch; paired deltas now come with a paired permutation p-value (new `paired_prediction_test`, BH-corrected), paired by sample rather than by fold; and `run_paired_permutation_assessment` refuses runs that were not scored on the same samples instead of silently testing their overlap.
