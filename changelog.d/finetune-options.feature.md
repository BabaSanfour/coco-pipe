`TrainerConfig` has four new fine-tuning options, all off by default: `lr_warmup_epochs` (linear warmup, then a cosine decay), `accumulate_grad_batches` (average the gradients of several batches before each optimizer step), `focal_gamma` (focal loss instead of cross-entropy for classification) and `probe_epochs` with `probe_lr` (train the head alone first, then train as `train_mode` describes).
