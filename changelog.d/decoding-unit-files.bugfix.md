A decoding unit now writes two files instead of up to sixteen: `result.joblib` and `run_manifest.json`. The twelve per-unit CSV tables, `config_used.yaml` and the `_SUCCESS` / `_FAILED` markers are gone: every table is read back from the result with `ExperimentResult.load(...).get_*()`, and the manifest holds the config, its hash, the status and the summary rows a resumed sweep needs. `ExperimentResult.export` no longer takes `formats`.
